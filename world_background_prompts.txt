================================================================
BUBBLE POP — GAME SCREEN BACKGROUND IMAGE PROMPTS (67 WORLDS)
================================================================

HOW TO USE
----------
- One prompt per world (the game has 67 worlds x 15 levels; all levels in a
  world share one background, tinted by the game at runtime).
- Every prompt is standalone: paste it directly into your image generator.
- If your generator supports a reference image, also attach
  assets/boba/chara1.png so "Boba the mascot" matches your real character.
- Recommended output: portrait 9:16, at least 1080x1920 (or 1024x1820).

GOLDEN RULES (already written into every prompt)
------------------------------------------------
1) READABILITY FIRST — the middle of the image stays soft, bright and almost
   empty. All scenery lives in the bottom third, the far horizon, and the
   side edges. Colorful game bubbles must stay easy to see on top of it.
2) CHARACTER CAMEO — Boba, the game's cute round bubble-tea pearl mascot with
   big sparkly eyes and tiny arms, appears SMALL in a bottom corner, never in
   the center.
3) NEXT-WORLD TEASER — a tiny glimpse of the NEXT world appears far away on
   the horizon (a silhouette, a glow, a signpost), so the player wonders
   "what is that place?" and keeps playing to reach it.
4) NO text, NO logos, NO UI elements, NO real-world brands.

NEGATIVE PROMPT (use if your tool has one)
------------------------------------------
text, watermark, logo, UI, buttons, busy center, high contrast center,
dark colors, photorealism, clutter, people, harsh shadows
